PTE – Summarize Written Text

← Back to Toolkit
The Method: 1) Pick one full, independent sentence from the passage and write it exactly as it is. 2) Replace its ending full stop with a comma. 3) Add a joining word (and / but / while / whereas). 4) Add another sentence from the passage, with its first letter lowercased. Repeat if needed until you're between 50–70 words. Capitalise the very first letter, end with one full stop, and never use a period anywhere in the middle.
