Guide — Describing a Normal Photo

Before You Start — Quick Scan

  1. Who's in the photo? (people, how many, what they're doing)
  2. Where is it happening? (setting / location)
  3. What's in the background? (objects, environment, extra detail)
  4. What's the overall mood? (busy, calm, festive, joyful, serious)

How to Fill Each Blank (same template as before)

Intro — "The image on the screen depicts ___" → name the general scene in one phrase
Body 1 — "It is evident that ___" → the main action or focus, who's doing what
Body 2 — "Conversely, ___" → a genuine contrast in the photo, or a second detail if there's no clear contrast
Body 3 — "Another interesting point is ___" → a background detail or minor element
Conclusion — "Overall, this image illustrates ___" → the overall mood or purpose

Worked Examples

Demo 1 — Family Picnic on the Beach

The image on the screen depicts a family enjoying a relaxed picnic together on a sandy beach. It is evident that the parents and their daughter are sitting close together, talking and smiling warmly. Conversely, two young boys are playing on their own a little further away in the sand. Another interesting point is the small sailing boats visible out on the calm sea. Overall, this image illustrates a happy and peaceful family day by the sea.

Demo 2 — Solar System Size Comparison

The image on the screen depicts a size comparison between several planets in our solar system. It is evident that Jupiter and Saturn stand out as the largest, with Saturn clearly showing its rings. Conversely, Earth appears quite small when placed next to these giant planets. Another interesting point is that each planet is clearly labelled with its name. Overall, this image illustrates just how different the planets are in size.

Demo 3 — Group at Sunset

The image on the screen depicts a group of people posing playfully on a hilltop at sunset. It is evident that around ten people are shown in lively, energetic poses against the sky. Conversely, the sky behind them glows brightly in shades of orange and yellow. Another interesting point is that a few people are holding hiking poles nearby. Overall, this image illustrates a joyful moment shared together outdoors.

Demo 4 — Teacher and Student in a Lab

The image on the screen depicts a teacher supervising a student during a science experiment. It is evident that the student is carefully pouring a red liquid into a beaker. Conversely, the teacher stands close by and watches without stepping in. Another interesting point is the extra flasks placed on the table nearby. Overall, this image illustrates a simple and focused learning moment.

Demo 5 — Old vs New Computer

The image on the screen depicts a clear comparison between an old and a modern computer. It is evident that the older machine is large and bulky, with visible tape reels. Conversely, the modern computer is slim, with just a screen, keyboard, and mouse. Another interesting point is how much smaller the design has become over time. Overall, this image illustrates how far computer technology has advanced.
